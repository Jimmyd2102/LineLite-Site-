Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026.

Line Lite A Telecom Company, LLC ("Line Lite," "we") runs linelite.net. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Job applications: the details you enter on our application form, such as your name, contact information, location, experience, and availability.
  • Bid requests: the company, contact, and project details you enter on our request-a-bid form.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

How we use it

We use application information only to evaluate applicants and contact them about employment. We use bid request information only to respond to the request and discuss the project. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored in a secured database with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, and are accessible only to Line Lite staff who need them. We keep submissions only as long as they're useful for hiring or business purposes.

Please don't send sensitive information

Do not include Social Security numbers, medical information, or other sensitive personal information in any form on this site.

Your choices

To ask what information we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email info@linelite.net.
