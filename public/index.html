Who We Are

Line Lite is a young, up-and-coming outside plant contractor with a chip on its shoulder and something to prove. Aerial is our specialty, but we cover the full job — new builds, maintenance, and break/fix. We're building our reputation one job at a time, earning the trust of the contractors and network owners we work for through skilled hands, a relentless work ethic, and a crew that actually cares about the quality of their work.

We reward drive. The harder you work, the more you learn — and the more you earn. We stay safe, push each other to grow, and build the networks that keep communities connected.

What We Do

Our Services

Aerial construction is our specialty — and we cover the rest of the job too: splicing, underground, new builds, long haul, maintenance, and emergency repair.

Our Specialty 01

Aerial Construction

Strand placement, fiber lashing, and overlash on the pole line — built clean, safe, and to spec on every span.

02

Fiber Splicing

Fusion splicing for new builds, cut-overs, and repairs — clean enclosures and low-loss splices.

03

Underground Fiber Pulling / Jetting

Pulling and jetting fiber through conduit to carry the network from the pole line underground.

04

New Build Fiber to the Home

FTTH builds from the main line to the drop, getting homes and businesses connected.

05

Long Haul Fiber Projects

Long-haul routes along rural and county roads, built mile after mile to the same standard.

06

Emergency Break & Fix

Cut fiber or damaged plant? We respond fast to restore service and make the permanent repair.

07

Storm Damage Repair

When storms take lines down, Line Lite crews roll out to get communities reconnected.

08

OSP Maintenance

Ongoing outside plant maintenance that keeps networks safe, clear, and in shape.

09

Project Management Assistance

Experienced field leadership to help plan, coordinate, and track your project from start to finish.
