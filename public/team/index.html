Co-Founder

James Danis

James got into the telecom industry at 18 and has worked nearly every side of outside plant since then. In Vermont he spent four years as Outside Plant Manager at Mansfield Community Fiber. He ran the network from design through build, splicing, and maintenance, and spliced fiber himself along the way. He completed the Southeast Lineman Training Center's communication linework program in 2023, holds multiple industry certifications, and worked as a lineman before co-founding Line Lite in 2025. Through Line Lite, he has led crews subcontracting on projects for Spectrum and Comcast networks.

James also co-founded Cable Ledger, a field and billing platform built for fiber and cable contractors. He designed it around problems he dealt with firsthand: daily production reports, rate codes, as-builts, and crew pay. Line Lite runs on it every day, so the crews on your project have real-time production tracking and clean documentation behind them.
